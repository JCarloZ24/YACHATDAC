### What we do not know

Most of this Country has never been studied. These are the gaps we would like filled, and the reason we keep the record in the first place.

**How old is the wall?** — Marra Wonga has never been scientifically dated. Mud wasp nests over some engravings could give minimum ages if anyone samples them.

**What lives here?** — Four recorders are logging species and a good deal of it is unidentified. There is no full list of what is on this property.

**What is in the ground?** — Fossil footprints, petrified trees, and what one visiting specialist thought might be dinosaur eggs. Almost none of it examined.

**What does fire-stick farming actually do?** — Two flux towers are measuring it. More work is welcome, particularly on soil carbon at depth.
