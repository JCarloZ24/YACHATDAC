# The narrative spine

*Decided 30 August 2026. The first of the three artefacts the motion brief asks for, and the
one the other two answer to.*

This is written **from the approved material**, not invented: the v3 copy drafts in
`docs/content/drafts/`, the section-by-section contract in `docs/design/lofi-spec.md`, and the
verbs recorded in decision F7. Where a line below is the community's own, it is quoted and
sourced. Nothing here is a plausible sentence written to fill a gap — on this subject an
invented detail is not a placeholder, it is a falsehood with their name on it.

---

## 1. Who is arriving, and what do they already believe?

Someone who thinks of this as a **closed chapter**. Aboriginal history in the past tense:
something that happened, to be read about, visited, or commemorated. They may be sympathetic.
They are still reading it as *over*.

The v3 draft answers them directly, in the footer, in the community's own rewrite:

> "Turraburra is Iningai Country. We are its Traditional Custodians, and we are still here."

That sentence is the whole disagreement the site exists to settle.

## 2. What is different about them at the bottom of the page?

> **They stop reading it as history and start standing in a present tense.**

Not "they are informed". The change is one of **tense**. They arrive treating the Iningai as a
subject in the past and leave understanding that the floor under everything they just scrolled
is still held, still current, and still being worked.

Truth argues this structurally rather than rhetorically, and the argument is already built:

> "Lore is not a date. It is the floor everything above has been resting on the whole way down."

That is the sentence the motion work exists to deliver. Every choice in `motion-grammar.md` is
answerable to it.

## 3. What single question is the site answering?

**Not** *what happened to the Iningai people?* — that question keeps the reader in the past
tense and the site would be answering it for the rest of its life.

**Instead:** *what is still here, and who holds it?*

The three decided verbs are three answers to that one question: Home **opens**, Truth
**descends**, Living Work **accumulates**.

## 4. The acts

Five. An act is a movement, not a page — two of these live inside one route.

| | Act | What it does to the reader | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | **The opening** | Withholds. No navigation exists until section 05, so the reader cannot browse away from the argument before it is made — withholding navigation *is* the page's argument (D24). | Home 01–04 |
| **II** | **The descent** | Takes them down through the chronology, era by era, the ground darkening as the rail counts back. | Truth 00 → the escarpment |
| **III** | **The hard stop** | Stops them. No rail, no markers, nothing else on screen, and no way past it. The viewport is held. | Truth, the hard stop |
| **IV** | **The floor** | Lands. Underneath the last date is a thing that is not a date. | Truth, engraving → Wattanuri |
| **V** | **The work continuing** | Turns the past tense over: this is a going concern, and here is what is being done. | Living Work, then the Invitation |

## 5. The turn

**Act III — the hard stop on Truth.** It is already specified, and it is already in the right
place: the descent runs down through 1840s and the escarpment, and then the page simply stops
being a page.

Everything above the hard stop is the reader moving through a record. Everything below it is
the reader being addressed. That is the direction change, and the lo-fi's own spec is blunt
about the mechanism: *"no rail, no markers, nothing else on screen, and no way past it."*

Rail A stops there. Rail B resumes below it and ends at Wattanuri rather than running to the
foot of the page — because if the rail *is* the descent, it must not continue past the floor.

## 6. The last thing seen, and the one thing asked

**Last seen:** the Invitation — the three ways in (Wonder, Truth, Living Work), the first
navigation the homepage has offered.

**The one thing asked:** *Get in touch* → `/connect`. One ask, one destination.

The newsletter field is inert until R9 clears, so it is not the ask and must not be dressed as
one. There is no enquiry form on Truth; `#partner` resolves to the Partnerships card, which
also leaves for `/connect`.

## The single biggest fork

> **Scroll advances time in the Truth descent, in Home's A2 pinned beats (02–04), and at
> Living Work's spring. Scroll reveals everywhere else.**

The brief forbids "both" as an answer, and rightly — a site that mixes the two arbitrarily is
the kind that feels confused. So this names the exceptions and why they are exceptions:

- **Truth's descent** — the rail encodes a chronology and has done since the first screen. The
  reader running the projector *is* the reader moving through time. Scrub is not a flourish
  here; it is the subject.
- **Home's beats 02–04** — the A2 sky clock literally is time. One pin, 417vh, three beats on
  one clock at 0.00 / 0.33 / 0.66.
- **Living Work §05, THE SPRING** — added 2026-08-30 from that page’s hi-fi, which says it
  outright: "the only place scroll controls time". Eight days a spring ran, counted one per step
  and snapped. It earns the exception for the same reason as the other two — the passing of those
  eight days *is* the content, not a way of presenting it.

Everywhere else the page is a page and things arrive as they come into view. Cheap, robust, and
it keeps the three time-scrub moments rare enough to still mean something.

One distinction keeps this honest as the site gets denser: plenty of screens are **scrubbed** —
grounds ramp, plates parallax, apertures open — but only these three use scroll to advance
**time**. Scrubbing a texture is not the same as scrubbing a chronology, and only the second one
is the fork this section answers.

Two things, and neither is a gap in this spine:

- **The Welcome to Country wording (R1)** — the v3 text is marked "[DRAFT for Suzanne to
  correct or replace]" and says on its face that it is a statement of custodianship, *not* an
  Acknowledgement. Her words are outstanding.

Both are copy that is not ours to write. The spine is complete without them, and the motion
work is not blocked by either.
