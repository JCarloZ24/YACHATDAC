## The three rules that decide most picks

**1 · Batch 2 is a *guiding* shoot.** Nearly every frame contains visitors, because that is
what the shoot was. Ask *who is the subject*, not *is a staff member present*. Twelve of
seventeen photographs on the first Living Work hi-fi came from batch 2 and the page read as
a tourism page. Every batch-2 entry carries a **Crop** line — most frames can be cropped
into a work frame.

**2 · Batch 1 is an elder / knowledge shoot.** A portrait from it captioned under "Iningai
Rangers" asserts that person is a ranger. Keep captions describing *what the frame shows*,
never who the person is, and hold identification alongside consent.

**3 · Batch 3 is fire, and fire is a practice.** Fire on this site means fire-stick farming
— a responsibility, not weather and not atmosphere. The trap is beauty: `fire-line-duskblue`,
`fire-night-silhouette`, `fire-line-sunset` and `fire-horizon-night` are the most spectacular
pictures in the collection and they will be reached for as heroes on pages that have nothing
to do with fire. A fire frame belongs beside copy about caring for Country and nowhere else.
Several of them also read as *wildfire* to anyone who does not know the practice — those
carry a caption requirement on their entries.
