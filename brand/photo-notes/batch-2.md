39 frames, a **guiding shoot** — staff in branded clothing hosting a visiting family.
Masters 2000px on the long edge (24 landscape, 7 portrait), PNG.
Figma: `PHOTO LIBRARY · 2nd batch`, node `2049:12760`, children `2049:12721`–`12759`.
Local: `brand/photography/batch-2/`.

## Read this before picking anything from batch 2

**Nearly every frame contains visitors, because that is what the shoot was.** Ask *who is
the subject*, not *is a staff member present*. Twelve of seventeen photographs on the first
Living Work hi-fi came from this batch and the page read as a tourism page.

Every entry below carries a **Who** line and a **Crop** line. The crop line is the useful
one: most frames can be cropped into a work frame by removing the visitors, and several can
be cropped to remove children entirely.

**Children appear in 15 frames.** Where a child's face is identifiable, guardian consent is
required regardless of the crop.

**Branded clothing is legible in many frames** — the wordmark and "…Walking Together with
Co…" tagline read clearly on several tees. That ties the wearer to the organisation.
