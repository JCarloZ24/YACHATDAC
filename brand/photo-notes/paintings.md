# Paintings — three photographed works

Three photographs of **original dot paintings**, each shot on a spiral-bound sketchbook or
pad. These are photographs of physical artworks, not vector exports — a different asset
class from `brand/artwork/vectors/`, and the only full-colour artwork the project holds.

Figma: loose nodes right of `PHOTO LIBRARY · 2nd batch` — `2582:19778`, `2582:19779`,
`2582:19780`. Local: `brand/artwork/paintings/` (tracked in git; they are brand assets, not
client media).

⚠ **The Figma file records no artist for these.** The filenames are social-media CDN
strings and carry no attribution. **Do not credit anyone until it is confirmed** — logged in
OPEN QUESTIONS in `docs/ART-DIRECTION.md`.

⚠ **All three need cropping to use.** Each shows the spiral binding, and two show the
surface they were photographed on (a desk edge, a green couch). Cropping to the artwork is
a framing decision, not a change to the work.

⚠ **Their palettes are not the brand palette.** The turquoise, violet and red-orange grounds
are the artist's, not `YACHATDAC Colour`. Do not recolour them to match — §6 and the
standing cultural rules both bind here, and recolouring an artwork misrepresents it.

### painting-01-turquoise
- **File:** `painting-01-turquoise.jpg` · 1448×1086 · JPEG · 341KB · landscape
- **Subject:** Dense dot painting on a turquoise-blue ground. Vertical dotted columns run the
  full height; a large arch or dome form sits left of centre, built from concentric dotted
  lines that step inward to a solid core; a strong horizontal wave band of larger white dots
  crosses the lower third; a field of graduated dots fills the upper right.
- **Palette:** Turquoise ground `#1A8FD0`, deeper blue shadow `#0E5E92`, white and cream dots
  `#EDEDE4`, pale blue mid-tone `#5FB3E0`.
- **Composition:** Fills the frame edge to edge — a true all-over pattern with no empty
  ground. The arch is the only singular form; everything else is repetition.
- **Frame condition:** Spiral binding along the left edge; slight perspective skew, shot at
  a small angle rather than flat-on.
- **Use:** The strongest of the three as a **repeating band or texture field** — pattern
  bands as section dividers, or a large-scale reveal. The horizontal wave band crops out
  cleanly as a standalone divider strip.

### painting-02-purple
- **File:** `painting-02-purple.jpg` · 1149×1369 · JPEG · 181KB · **portrait**
- **Subject:** A central vertical oval outlined in a ring of large pearl-white dots, filled
  with a branching network of fine dotted lines. Dotted rays radiate outward from the oval to
  small concentric rosettes scattered across the field. The ground is a violet storm sky with
  visible lightning branches; a silhouetted eucalypt treeline runs along the bottom edge.
- **Palette:** Violet-purple ground `#5B3A7E`, magenta-lilac glow `#9B6BB5`, gold-ochre
  rosettes `#D9A227`, pearl white `#E8E4E8`, near-black treeline `#1A1420`.
- **Composition:** Strongly centred and radial. The treeline gives it a horizon, so it reads
  as a scene rather than a pattern — the only one of the three that does.
- **Frame condition:** Spiral binding along the top edge; photographed on what appears to be
  a green cloth surface, visible at the edges.
- **Use:** ⭐ **The most complete composition of the three.** Portrait orientation and a
  built-in horizon make it a candidate for a full-height panel or a night/sky beat. The
  radial structure suits a slow scale reveal. ⚠ Its violet is entirely outside the brand
  palette — it will not sit quietly beside the Country colours, so give it its own ground.

### painting-03-red
- **File:** `painting-03-red.jpg` · 1448×1086 · JPEG · 234KB · landscape
- **Subject:** A radial burst on a red-to-orange graded ground. A central rosette sits at the
  midpoint; white meandering lines radiate outward in every direction, each terminating in or
  passing through concentric dot rosettes in gold and white. Roughly twenty rosettes of
  varying size fill the field.
- **Palette:** Deep red ground `#C42B18`, orange lower ground `#E4661B`, gold rosette centres
  `#F2B33C`, white line work `#F4EDE2`.
- **Composition:** Symmetrical and centred, energy pushing outward from the middle. Reads
  well at any scale and stays legible when small.
- **Frame condition:** Spiral binding along the left edge; photographed on a green couch,
  visible along the bottom and right.
- **Use:** ⭐ The most **graphically robust** of the three — the radial structure survives
  heavy cropping and scales down to a card or a motif. Its red-orange sits closest to the
  brand's Burnt Ochre and Oxide Red, so it integrates most easily. Good for a centred hero
  motif or a section anchor.

## What may be done with these

Motion on supplied artwork is **permitted** — Ivy, 2026-08-30 — and that permission was
extended on 2026-08-31. The standing limit is unchanged and applies here:

**Animating, masking, revealing, cropping and recomposing a supplied work is permitted.
Authoring new geometry in that style is not.** These are photographs of paintings, so
"recompose" means crop, mask, scale, layer and reveal the photograph — not trace it, not
redraw a rosette, not generate a matching motif.

Leonard Mickelo's own artist sign-off remains outstanding in
`.claude/skills/yachatdac-motion/references/permissions.md`, and attribution for these three
works specifically is unconfirmed.
